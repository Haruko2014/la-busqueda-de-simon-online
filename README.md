# La Búsqueda de Simón Online

**An evolving fantasy MMORPG with creative-writing education and AI-assisted storytelling.**

> **Project status:** Early development. Features described below are part of the project's vision and roadmap; their availability may vary. Check the repository source and releases for implemented functionality.

## Overview

La Búsqueda de Simón Online is an independent browser-based fantasy game project combining exploration, character progression, quests, combat, social interaction, and player-driven storytelling. A central educational goal is to help learners practice creative writing through meaningful in-game activities rather than isolated exercises.

Players will be encouraged to write character biographies, invent stories, complete narrative challenges, develop dialogue, and contribute to a shared fantasy world.

## Project goals

- **Make writing engaging:** Integrate short, achievable creative-writing challenges into quests and role-playing.
- **Support creativity and literacy:** Encourage narrative structure, vocabulary, dialogue, revision, and imagination.
- **Build an immersive game:** Develop a persistent medieval fantasy world with classes, skills, NPCs, exploration, equipment, crafting, and PvE/PvP systems.
- **Explore responsible AI:** Prototype AI-assisted NPC dialogue, adaptive quests, writing feedback, and storytelling support with appropriate safeguards.
- **Enable community contributions:** Document the project and invite constructive feedback and collaboration.

## Educational writing experience

The proposed writing-learning loop is designed to be approachable:

1. **Discover a story:** A character or quest introduces a narrative problem.
2. **Write to progress:** The player creates dialogue, a short story, a description, or a decision with supporting reasons.
3. **Receive feedback:** Clear guidance helps the player improve coherence, vocabulary, and creativity without replacing their own voice.
4. **Revise and share:** Players can improve their work and, where appropriate, choose to contribute it to the game world.

Activities may be adapted to different ages and skill levels. Any child-facing experience should use age-appropriate moderation, privacy protections, and human oversight where necessary.

## Potential OpenAI API integrations

API credits, if awarded, would support exploration and prototyping of:

- Context-aware NPC conversations and story interactions.
- Dynamic narrative quests and creative-writing prompts.
- Writing feedback for clarity, structure, and grammar.
- Personalized learning pathways and multilingual assistance.
- Content moderation and safer user-generated storytelling workflows.

**AI features are proposed integrations, not a claim that they are currently deployed.** API access, usage limits, costs, safety testing, and privacy requirements will be evaluated before production deployment.
